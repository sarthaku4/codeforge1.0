The Institution & Heritage

A historic legacy rooted in educational empowerment and technological leadership

Janata Shikshan Prasarak Mandal (JSPM)

Babasaheb Naik College of Engineering (BNCOE), Pusad was founded in 1983 under the visionary aegis of Janata Shikshan Prasarak Mandal (JSPM). Established by the towering visionary and former Chief Minister of Maharashtra, Late Shri Vasantraoji Naik, and former Minister Late Shri Babasaheb Naik, the trust was envisioned to bring world-class engineering education to the heart of rural Maharashtra.

Over four decades, the college has transformed into one of Central India's premier technical institutions, producing top-notch engineers, innovators, startup founders, and industry leaders across Fortune 500 companies globally.

Approvals & Accreditations

  • AICTE Approved: Recognized by All India Council for Technical Education, New Delhi.
  • SGBAU Affiliated: Permanently affiliated with Sant Gadge Baba Amravati University.
  • NBA & NAAC Accredited: High accreditation credentials with Grade "A" status by the Govt. of Maharashtra.
  • PhD Research Centers: Recognized research centers in Computer Science, Civil, and Mechanical Engineering.

Campus Infrastructure & Amenities

Spread across a lush green 30-acre campus designed for learning, research, and holistic student life

Central Digital Library

Houses over 60,000+ volumes, rare technical manuscripts, DELNET subscriptions, and access to premier international journals including IEEE Xplore, ASME, ASCE, Springer, and ScienceDirect, plus reading halls with 300+ seating.

High-Performance Computing

Over 1,000+ computer workstations connected to a 1 Gbps high-speed optical fiber network, dedicated GPU computation servers for AI/ML tasks, and round-the-clock Wi-Fi across the entire academic complex.

Residential Hostels

Separate on-campus hostels for boys and girls with furnished rooms, 24/7 security & CCTV surveillance, hygienic mess serving nutritious food, solar water heating, study rooms, and recreational lounges.

Sports & Fitness Arena

Spacious athletic grounds featuring full-size cricket and football pitches, basketball and volleyball courts, indoor badminton courts, table tennis, and a modern equipped multi-gym for student physical fitness.

Auditoriums & Seminar Halls

Centrally air-conditioned 500+ capacity main auditorium along with multiple smart A/V seminar halls for hosting national hackathons, international conferences, expert guest lectures, and cultural events.

IIC & Startup Incubation

Ministry of Education's Institution's Innovation Council (IIC), dedicated startup incubator, patent facilitation cell, and active student chapters including CSI, IEEE, ISTE, and ACM.
