About The Event

Pure Engineering Adrenaline

CodeForge brings together the brightest student minds from colleges across Maharashtra. Teams of 4 tackle real-world problem statements across software, hardware, and design tracks — judged by industry professionals.

Whether you're a coder, designer, or strategist — there's a role for you. Arrive with an idea. Leave with a product.

How The Day Unfolds

1
Day 0

Registration & Check-In

2
09:00 AM

Problem Reveal & Kickoff

Problem statements are revealed. The clock starts. Non-stop hacking begins.

3
Mid-Day
